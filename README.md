# ГӀалгӀайче · Ghalghaiche

A flight through the mountains of Ingushetia on a moonlit evening, right in your browser: a glacial river in a deep gorge, alpine meadows and pine woods, the flat-topped Table Mountain, the snows of the main Caucasus ridge, and the stone towers the Ingush built in these valleys.

The whole world is generated by code each time the page loads. It has no images, 3D models or sound files and uses no game engine. It is drawn directly with **WebGPU**, its shaders are written in WGSL, and its sound is synthesised live with the Web Audio API. Everything ships in a single `index.html` of about 150 KB.

## Try it

Download `index.html` and open it in a browser with WebGPU: a recent Chrome or Edge, or Safari 26+. Click **Enter**.

## What's there

- **Battle towers (vov)**: tall, tapering stone towers with stepped pyramid roofs of slate slabs, a cone keystone, machicolation balconies and slit windows
- **Residential towers (gala)**: broad, flat-roofed and lower, many of them ruined shells
- **Crypts (kashi)**: family tombs with stepped roofs, clustered on a slope like a small city of the dead
- Lone **signal towers** along the gorge with flat roofs and corner horns, where fires burn after dark. Fires were once passed from tower to tower to warn the valley
- A village mosque in Erzi: a stone hall under a hipped roof and an octagonal minaret with a crescent, its qibla wall turned toward Mecca
- An old **stone arch bridge**, **watermills** on the streams, and a shepherd's **sheepfold** with a hut and a campfire on a high meadow
- A **waterfall** cascading down the gorge wall, with spray where it lands
- A **mountain lake** in a high basin that mirrors the peaks, sky and shores (on Medium and High), and freezes in winter
- A **glacier** in a high cirque in the south, with crevasses and dark moraine edges, and its meltwater stream
- Warm firelight in the windows of Erzi after dark
- Dry-stone walls, terraced fields, footpaths and the road along the river
- A tributary canyon with towers raised on rock pillars
- **Kyazi**: a small castle of six residential towers strung along a ridge crest, mostly in ruins, joined by a defensive wall that closes a courtyard, with two crypts nearby and flocks of sheep grazing on its flanks
- **Koazoy-Loam**, the mountain of the Koazoy family: a ridge of the Rocky Range across the valley from Kyazi, breaking off in a sheer limestone wall with a ledge and scree below, its jagged crest a little higher than the Table Mountain
- Pine and birch woods, scree and cliffs and boulders
- Animals: sheep and horses grazing with shepherd dogs, East Caucasian tur on the cliffs, a wolf pack trotting the high meadows, eagles circling over the villages and bearded vultures soaring along the ridges
- **Animals** setting (Few, Normal, Many, Lots): Lots has about four times as many: more flocks with their dogs, horse herds, tur groups, wolf packs, eagles, vultures and bats
- Mist that drifts through the gorge in banks, heaviest at night and dawn
- **Avalanches**: now and then, on a slope that is under snow in the current season and in view, an avalanche breaks loose and runs down in a billowing cloud of powder; its deep rumble arrives late when it is far away, as sound would
- Fireflies over the meadows on warm nights, bats around the villages after dusk, butterflies by day, alpine flowers, and patches of Caucasian rhododendron with pale yellow blossoms on the high slopes
- **Seasons**: spring, summer, autumn (golden birches, straw-coloured meadows) and winter (snow down to the river, bare trees, a frozen lake)
- **Weather**: clear, rain, or changing, where clouds gather on the peaks, it rains (snows in winter) and clears again, with lightning and thunder in heavy rain and a rainbow when the sun comes out behind you
- A compact 4 km world: every village is a short flight away
- Evening by default: blue twilight, a warm afterglow in the west, stars, falling stars streaking across the sky now and then, and a large moon rising over the ridges. Dawn, morning, midday and golden hour are in the menu
- **Day & night**: an automatic cycle (16 minutes per day, slower around sunrise and sunset). The sun sets, stars and the moon come out, fires are lit, and dawn returns, with mountain shadows following the sun and moon

## Languages

English and Russian. A device set to Russian starts in Russian, others in English. Switch on the loading screen or under Language in the menu, or add `?lang=ru` / `?lang=en` to the link. All texts live in `source/src/i18n.js`.

Each place has a **checkpoint floating in the sky** above it. Click or tap one to fly there; when a ridge hides the place, its checkpoint dims. With the mouse captured, aim at a checkpoint and click.

Press **T** (or **Tour** on a phone) for a guided tour: a slow flight through all eight places with a pause at each. Any key or touch takes back control.

The places are inspired by real sites, but the geography is invented and compressed into a 4 km square.

## Detail levels

Low, Medium and High change render resolution, shadows, shader detail, grass, tree density and draw distance.

**Auto** (the default) adapts in both directions while you fly. Phones and tablets start on Low, desktops on Medium or High. If the frame rate stays low, the scene lowers its resolution, then drops a level. If it runs smoothly at 60 fps for a few seconds, it tries the next level up and keeps it only if it still holds about 60 fps. A level that proved too heavy isn't retried in that session. Picking Low, Medium or High in the menu pins that level instead.

URL parameters, mainly for testing: `?v=3` start at a viewpoint, `?t=dawn|morning|noon|golden|evening|cycle` (with `&hour=` for the cycle), `?season=spring|summer|autumn|winter`, `?weather=clear|changing|rain`, `?animals=few|normal|many|lots` set the time of day, `?q=low|medium|high` pin a detail level, `?stats` show stats, `?auto` skip the title screen, `?cam=x,y,z,yawDeg,pitchDeg` place the camera, `?avalanche` set off an avalanche right away, `?milkyway` preview the Milky Way in the night sky.

## How it works

- **Terrain**: a 1025 × 1025 heightfield (4 m spacing), built on the CPU from ridged and fractal simplex noise. It is designed in a larger space and scaled down (relief included) so the world stays compact with natural slopes. The river valleys are carved by blending that relief down toward each river bed with V- and U-shaped profiles. The Table Mountain is a plateau with a cliff band. Village sites are levelled and terraced afterwards. It is drawn as 256 tiles with three levels of detail, plus a far ring of mountains shaped in the vertex shader.
- **Lighting**: one key light casts the shadows: the sun by day, the moon in the evening. A compute shader marches the heightfield toward it to get soft mountain shadows, the height at which the sun becomes visible above each point (so tower tops catch the last light), and ambient occlusion. A shadow map around the camera adds shadows from towers, trees and rocks.
- **Sky and air**: an analytic sky with afterglow, stars, falling stars and a moon with maria and a halo, drifting clouds, a silhouette of the distant main range, height fog with an integral, and a thin valley mist.
- **Temporal upscaling**: the scene renders at 60–75% resolution with a different sub-pixel jitter each frame. A resolve pass reprojects last frame's full-resolution image using depth and the previous camera, blends in the new samples by how close each landed to the output pixel, and clamps the history to the current neighbourhood so moving things don't ghost. A light sharpen follows. This replaces MSAA and gives a sharp, anti-aliased image at a fraction of the cost, especially on high-density phone screens. `?taa=0` switches back to MSAA.
- **Lake reflection**: when the lake is near and in view, the terrain, distant mountains, towers and sky are drawn a second time, mirrored in the water plane, at half resolution, and the lake shows that image with ripples. Geometry below the water is clipped by a pipeline constant, so the normal pipelines carry no discard.
- **Grass**: tufts are generated in the vertex shader on a grid that follows the camera, so no instance buffer is needed.
- **Animals**: sheep graze in the vertex shader (head down and nibbling, then a few steps to fresh grass); eagles circle, glide and beat their wings.
- **Sound**: nature only. Wind, the river and the waterfall from filtered noise, rain and thunder, birdsong, an eagle's cry, the bleating of sheep and a crackling campfire. On iPhone and iPad it comes back when you return to Safari from the home screen (after a tap, if iOS insists).

## Make it your own

```sh
pnpm install
pnpm build            # minified index.html
pnpm build:readable   # unminified, easier to read
pnpm dev              # rebuild on every change
```

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `source/src/world.js` | Where villages, towers and crypts stand; tree, rock, flock and eagle scattering; viewpoints. **Start here.** |

| `source/src/audio.js` | Soundscape |
| `source/src/quality.js` | Detail levels and automatic fallback |
| `source/src/i18n.js` | English and Russian texts |
| `source/template.html` | Page layout, loading screen, menu and styles |
